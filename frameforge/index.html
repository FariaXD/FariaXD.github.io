FrameForge

Per-frame hitbox and hurtbox authoring for 2D combat in Unity. Draw boxes over your animation frames, set what they do on contact, and let a runtime component resolve the hits.

See the features Questions? Get in touch

No colliders to toggle. No events to forget.

The usual way to do combat in Unity is a pile of child objects with colliders, switched on and off by Animation Events. An interrupted move leaves a collider armed; a missing event means an attack that silently never connects.

FrameForge keeps hitboxes as data, drawn against the art itself, and resolves them with physics queries on exactly the frames they are live — so there is nothing to leave switched on.

Features

Draw on the art

Boxes are drawn over the sprite for each frame, read straight from your Animation Clip. Drag to move, grab a corner to resize.

Key a few frames

Boxes interpolate between keys — smoothly, or snapping with frame-by-frame pixel art. A wide sweep needs two keys, not twenty.

Hitboxes, hurtboxes, custom

Damage, knockback, hit stop and hit stun per box. Hurtboxes can reshape a character's hittable area during a move.

Your damage system

FrameForge reports hits and applies nothing itself. Handle them in code, or route them to your own functions in the Inspector by tag.

Follows the Animator

Optionally time attacks from the Animator, so speed changes and interrupted moves keep the boxes in step with the art.

Built for tuning

Keyboard shortcuts, copy and paste between frames and attacks, onion skin, undo, and a hover explanation on every setting.

What's included

  • The frame data editor window and inspector
  • Attack Player — the runtime component that resolves hits
  • Hurtbox Driver — reshapes a character's hittable collider during a move
  • A self-building demo you can drop into any scene, plus example scripts to learn from
  • Full source code, and automated tests including tests that run with physics
  • A full PDF manual
